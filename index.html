    The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule Explained

    • 50% Needs: Obligatory allocations including housing rent, bills, insurance, and groceries.
    • 30% Wants: Discretionary spending targeting entertainment, dining out, and streaming memberships.
    • 20% Savings: Asset accumulation including emergency accounts and investment portfolios.

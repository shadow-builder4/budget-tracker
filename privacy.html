Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information received, processed, and collected by Smart Wallet Tracker Pro and how it is managed.

Local Client-Side Storage Policy

All transactional flows, financial logs, and configuration inputs recorded within this application are processed entirely on the client-side using your browser's native localStorage database layers. No financial records, data logs, or income strings are ever synchronized, transmitted, or broadcast to external cloud servers, remote databases, or third-party tracking networks.

Google AdSense and Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve contextual advertisements across our informational resource subpages. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads to our users based on their visits to this site and other platforms on the Internet. Users can manage or disable custom tracking configurations by visiting the official Google Ad settings panel.

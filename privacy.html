Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Smart Wallet Tracker Pro. We value your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy details how our personal budgeting platform handles information securely within your web browser environment.

1. Information Collection and Storage Architecture

Unlike traditional personal banking apps, Smart Wallet Tracker Pro operates as a decentralized, static dashboard script.

  • Zero Server Storage: Your entry items, financial labels, categories, and numerical parameters are never sent to external servers, cloud databases, or our web hosting systems.
  • Client-Side Local Storage: All transaction records are locked entirely within your device's web browser framework using standard HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage). Your financial information stays under your physical ownership at all times.

2. Use of Information

Because your transaction entry history stays local, the operational logic inside our script uses your data solely to execute interactive equations directly on your display screen. This includes rendering user statistics, visual 50/30/20 progression bars, survival runway evaluations, and dynamic contextual optimization guidance boxes.

3. Third-Party Advertisement Integrations (Google AdSense)

We work with third-party service entities like Google AdSense to display ads when you visit our tracker platform. Google may use cookie metrics (such as the DoubleClick cookie tool) based on your visits to this and alternative online locations to show relevant ads tailored to your general web browsing preferences.

You can choose to opt out of tailored advertising setups by managing your preferences directly inside the Google Ads Settings interface.

4. Data Purging Rights and Controls

You retain absolute control over your tracking profile database. You can instantly wipe out your registry file records at any time by taking either of the following steps:

  • Clicking the "Reset All Data" button positioned at the bottom of the interface form panel.
  • Clearing your web browser's data storage cache within your browser application settings.

5. Security of Information

Since your information is stored directly inside your browser cache, the structural safety of your tracking profile depends on the device security of your computer or smartphone. We advise keeping your browser application up to date to ensure your data stays safe from unauthorized local access.

6. Changes to this Policy Statement

We may make minor tweaks to this privacy statement over time to stay aligned with new browser parameters or advertising updates. Any system refinements will update the active version directly on this layout view.

If you have any questions regarding local processing safety parameters, you can reach out via our open-source codebase profile directory at shadow-builder4.github.io.
